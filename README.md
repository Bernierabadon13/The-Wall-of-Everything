# The Wall of Everything
- I am lonely/Introvert person that can't tell words to others so, I made this web 3d roblox sorrounding to spit the words I want to say, Not just for me but for anybody who visit.

> Some things are easier to write than to say.

A peaceful 3D world with one giant public wall. Walk across a golden grass field at sunset, choose an empty spot on the wall, and leave something you never said. Your words appear exactly where you clicked, and fade away after 7 days.

## Message
- Every message you wrote will be visible to anyone.
- Your message is only visible for a week after that it will gone.

## Features
- First-person 3D world built with Three.js: walk, look, sprint, zoom
- Click anywhere on the wall: a raycaster finds the exact spot and saves it as wall coordinates (u, v), so every message reappears where it was written
- Type a confession (max 200 characters) or write it by hand on a pad
- Five writing styles (marker, chalk, spray paint, pen, graffiti), six handwriting fonts, eight colors, three sizes
- Live preview on the wall that blocks overlapping messages
- Click a message: it glows and the author's name bounces up and fades
- Messages expire after 7 days
- Human check (captcha), hidden bot trap and posting cooldown
- Dark golden sunset mood, swaying grass, circling birds that whistle back what you click, visible wind
- Synthesized sound (no audio files): bird calls and wind, press M to mute

## Controls
| Desktop | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Walk |
| Mouse | Look (drag if the mouse is not captured) |
| Shift | Sprint |
| Scroll | Zoom |
| Click | Choose a spot / read a message |
| Esc | Release the mouse |
| M | Sound on or off |

Mobile: left thumb walks, drag to look, pinch to zoom, tap the wall.
