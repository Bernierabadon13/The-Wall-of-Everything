# The Wall of Everything

## Project Ownership & Authorship

**The Wall of Everything** is an independently developed interactive web project. Its concept, creative direction, design decisions, and overall project vision are owned and directed by the original creator.

This project transforms the traditional online confession wall into an immersive 3D first-person experience where people can explore an open environment and leave personal messages on a shared public wall.

- **Original Concept:** Original project concept and direction by the project owner.
- **Project Ownership:** All original project materials and creative decisions belong to the project owner.
- **Development:** Independently managed and directed by the project owner, with AI-assisted development where applicable.

## Project Vision

The goal is to create a shared digital space where people can express themselves without needing to reveal their identities.

Visitors can explore a peaceful 3D environment, approach a central wall, choose their preferred location, and leave a confession, thought, memory, or message.

Every message becomes part of a collective space created by its visitors.

## Original Creative Direction

The project is guided by the following principles:

1. **Freedom of Expression** – Everyone should have the freedom to leave their thoughts on the wall.
2. **Shared Experience** – Every visitor contributes to a continuously growing collection of messages.
3. **Immersion** – The experience should feel like entering a small interactive world rather than browsing a conventional website.
4. **Simplicity** – The interface should remain clean, accessible, and easy to understand.
5. **Authenticity** – Every message represents an individual contribution to the wall.
